# MORNING BRIEF spec

When the user says "morning briefing", "executive briefing" or "daily brief", give them these three parts, in this order. Keep it short and scannable. Delivered around 8 AM IST.

## 1. Most important email
Check their Gmail inboxes (all connected accounts). Tell them the single most important email: who it is from, what it says in two lines, why it matters. Then write a suggested reply they can approve. Mention other notable mail in one line each. Never send a reply without their OK.

## 2. LinkedIn
- Urgent messages: list anything urgent with a drafted reply for their OK.
- Who was wished: birthdays, promotions and new jobs of their connections who were wished.
- Engagement tally: a count of the day's engagement (likes, comments, wishes, new connections, followers) compared with yesterday.

## 3. News
- Top 10 news from India that he should know (business, tech, AI, politics, law).
- 5 international news items.
- One line each, with the source, and a link for the top story.

## Rules
- Use only what FRIDAY can actually reach. If email or LinkedIn is not connected on this PC, say plainly "I cannot reach your Gmail/LinkedIn from here" and still give the news. Never invent emails, messages or numbers.
- News must come from a live web search, not memory. Say the date.
- Start with "Good morning".
